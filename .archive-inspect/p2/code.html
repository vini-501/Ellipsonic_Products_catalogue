The Problem

Spoken explanation without aligned visuals increases confusion, slows learning, and leads to repeated clarifications. Producing live, labeled diagrams at scale requires human time and expertise.

The Solution

Chalk AI pairs continuous voice interaction with a live, programmatic whiteboard driven by a real‑time multimodal model. The model issues structured drawing commands (shapes, paths, labelled equations & axes) while speaking; the browser renders visuals in sync. Natural interruption (barge‑in) and sentence‑level captions preserve conversational flow.
